---
title: MDX When You Need It
description: Use Markdown for most posts and MDX only when a post needs a component.
pubDate: 2026-09-06
tags:
  - mdx
  - workflow
---

Most posts should stay as plain Markdown. It is portable, readable in GitHub, and quick to edit.

MDX is useful when the writing needs a live component, a custom callout, or a small interactive example.

```js
const rule = 'Default to Markdown. Reach for MDX when the post earns it.';
```

That rule keeps the blog from becoming an app before it needs to.
